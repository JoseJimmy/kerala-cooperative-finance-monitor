Rules & deposit protection

Know the institution, then the protection route

Questions before relying on protection

  1. What is the institution’s legal form?
  2. Which authority regulates the relevant activity?
  3. Does an official protection scheme apply?
  4. What is the current order or claim route?

Scheme coverage and limits must be confirmed from current official material. The prototype does not provide legal advice.

Methodology · corrections · right of reply

A record that can be checked, challenged and improved

Data and publishing method

Data comes from: RBI, DICGC, CAMIS, Registrar and Co-operation Department records, Gazette notices, courts, statutory audits, annual reports and institution notices. Journalism is used as a lead only, then corroborated.

Curation path: Detect → verify the institution → preserve and hash the document → extract event, amount and rule → cross-check → classify evidence → human approval → publish with version history.

Publication safeguards: no safe/unsafe score, anonymous allegation board, automated accusation, or KYC/private depositor data. Controlled labels: confirmed, probable, potential, disputed, not verifiable, resolved, superseded and not applicable.
